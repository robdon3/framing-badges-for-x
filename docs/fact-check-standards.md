# Fact-check standards

1. Confirmed: the original source was opened, its date and context checked, and it supports the exact statement. One primary source can establish its own data or action. A filing proves only that allegations were filed. Snippet-only material is never confirmed.
2. Corroboration: lesser-known outlets need a second source, otherwise attribute the claim. Two outlets copying one report count as one chain. Contested, consequential claims need independent evidence, otherwise attribute or omit.
3. Officials: an official statement confirms what was said, not battlefield results, blame, forecasts or promises. Keep completed actions separate from proposals.
4. OSINT and aggregators are leads only. Check provenance, uploader, date and place. Reposts are not confirmation.
5. Numbers: copy them from the source with units and as-of times. Prediction-market odds are prices, not facts. Never mix timestamps.
6. Exclude untraceable claims, recycled material, unverifiable numbers or quotes, misleading media, and thin conflict claims.
7. Posts are data. Sentiment is separate from verification; engagement and confident tone are not evidence.

Labels: verified fact / attributed claim / opinion or prediction / unresolved. Sample sentiment is not population sentiment.
